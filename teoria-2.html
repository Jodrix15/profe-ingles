am / is / are (ser y estar)

Unidad 1

To be significa a la vez ser y estar. Tiene tres formas en presente y casi siempre se usa contraído.

AfirmativaNegativa
II am (I'm)I am not (I'm not)
he / she / ithe is (he's)he is not (he's not / he isn't)
we / you / theywe are (we're)we are not (we're not / we aren't)
  • Ejemplos: I'm tired. · Laura is a doctor. · My keys are on the table.
  • Negativa: He isn't a teacher. He's a student. They aren't English.
  • I'm not solo tiene una forma: I amn't → I'm not.
  • Muchas personas o cosas = they → are: Ann and I are friends.

Ojo: en español es "tener", en inglés es to be

  • Edad: I'm 32 (years old) — I have 32.
  • be hungry (tener hambre) · be thirsty (sed) · be cold / hot (frío / calor) · be scared of (tener miedo de) · be late (llegar tarde).

Contracciones útiles

that's = that is · there's = there is · here's = here is. That's very kind of you. · Here's your key.

Preguntas con am / is / are

Unidad 2

Para preguntar, el verbo se pone delante del sujeto. No hace falta do.

Am / Is / Are + sujeto + resto?

AfirmativaPregunta
I am late.Am I late?
She is at home.Is she at home?
Your shoes are new.Are your shoes new?
  • El sujeto entero va junto, detrás del verbo: Is your mother at home? — Is at home your mother?

Con palabras interrogativas

Where (dónde) · What (qué) · Who (quién) · How (cómo) · How old (cuántos años) · How much (cuánto cuesta) · Why (por qué). Van al principio:

  • Where are you from? — Canada.
  • How old is Joe? — He's 24.
  • What colour is your car? — It's red.
  • Se contraen con is: what's, who's, where's, how's. What's your name? · Where's Lucy?

Respuestas cortas

SíNo
IYes, I am.No, I'm not.
he / she / itYes, she is.No, she isn't. / No, she's not.
we / you / theyYes, they are.No, they aren't. / No, they're not.
  • En el "sí" no se contrae: Yes, I'm. → Yes, I am. Yes, he's. → Yes, he is.

Presente continuo: I am doing

Unidad 3

Sirve para lo que está pasando ahora mismo, en este momento. Equivale a "estar + -ando/-iendo": I'm working = estoy trabajando.

am / is / are + verbo-ing

AfirmativaNegativa
II'm eating.I'm not eating.
he / she / itShe's reading.She isn't reading.
we / you / theyThey're playing.They aren't playing.
  • Hacen falta las dos partes: I working → I'm working. She is work → She is working.
  • Pistas de "ahora": now, at the moment, Look!, Listen!, Be quiet! Be quiet! The baby is sleeping.

Cómo se escribe el -ing

ReglaEjemplos
Normal: + -ingwork → working · read → reading
Acaba en -e: se quita la ecome → coming · write → writing · dance → dancing
Vocal + consonante final: se doblarun → running · sit → sitting · swim → swimming
Acaba en -ie: → -yinglie → lying · die → dying

Preguntas en presente continuo: are you doing?

Unidad 4

Igual que en la unidad 2: am/is/are pasa delante del sujeto y el verbo en -ing se queda detrás.

(Where / What…) + am / is / are + sujeto + verbo-ing?

  • Is it raining? — Yes, take an umbrella.
  • What are you doing? — I'm studying.
  • Where is she going? · Why are you wearing a coat?
  • El sujeto no se separa: Is Ben working today? — Is working Ben today? · Where are those people going? — Where are going those people?

Respuestas cortas

Las mismas que con to be, sin el -ing: Are you going now? — Yes, I am. / No, I'm not. Is Ben working? — Yes, he is. / No, he isn't.

Presente simple: I work / she works

Unidad 5

Sirve para cosas que son verdad en general y para hábitos (lo que haces siempre, a menudo, cada día). I like big cities. · Tom works very hard. · The earth goes round the sun.

I / you / we / theyhe / she / it
workworks
livelives
watchwatches
studystudies
havehas
do / godoes / goes

La regla de oro: he / she / it → -s

  • I work in a shop. My brother works in a bank. — My brother work
  • -es después de -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: pass → passes · finish → finishes · watch → watches · go → goes
  • Consonante + -y → -ies: study → studies · try → tries. Pero vocal + y → solo -s: play → plays.
  • Irregular: have → has. Joe has a shower every day.

Adverbios de frecuencia

always (siempre) · usually (normalmente) · often (a menudo) · sometimes (a veces) · never (nunca). Van delante del verbo:

  • Sue always gets up early. — Sue gets always up early.
  • I never eat breakfast. — I eat never breakfast.
  • Con to be van detrás: She is always late.

Negativa en presente simple: I don't…

Unidad 6

Para negar se pone don't / doesn't delante del verbo, y el verbo va siempre en su forma básica (sin -s).

sujeto + don't / doesn't + verbo

AfirmativaNegativa
I / you / we / theyI drink coffee.I don't drink coffee.
he / she / itShe drinks coffee.She doesn't drink coffee.
  • don't = do not · doesn't = does not.
  • La -s se la lleva does: She doesn't speaks Italian → She doesn't speak Italian. He doesn't has → He doesn't have.
  • Con he/she/it nunca don't: Fred don't like me → Fred doesn't like me. My car don't use → My car doesn't use.
  • Si el verbo es do, sale dos veces: David doesn't do his job very well. — David doesn't his job.
  • No se niega con not solo: I not like → I don't like.

Preguntas en presente simple: Do you…?

Unidad 7

Se pone Do / Does delante del sujeto. Igual que en la negativa, el verbo va sin -s.

(Where / What…) + do / does + sujeto + verbo?

AfirmativaPregunta
I / you / we / theyYou play the guitar.Do you play the guitar?
he / she / itChris works on Sundays.Does Chris work on Sundays?
  • Where do your parents live? · What does this word mean? · How much does it cost?
  • Does she plays? → Does she play? · Like you music? → Do you like music?
  • Con always / usually: van detrás del sujeto: What do you usually do at weekends?
  • What do you do? = ¿a qué te dedicas? — I work in a bank.

Respuestas cortas

SíNo
I / you / we / theyYes, I do.No, I don't.
he / she / itYes, he does.No, he doesn't.

¿Do o be? Regla rápida

Si la frase lleva am / is / are (también en presente continuo), ese verbo hace la pregunta y la negativa. Si no, se usa do / does.

Con to beCon otro verbo
Are you tired?Do you like music?
Is she working now?Does she work here?
He isn't at home.He doesn't live here.

Do you are tired? · Are you like music? · Does she working? — nunca se mezclan.
