⏱ ¿Qué tiempo uso? Palabras pista
Si ves…Usa…Ejemplo
now, Look!, Shh!Presente continuoLook! It's raining.
every day, usually, alwaysPresente simpleI work on Saturdays.
yesterday, last…, … ago, in 2020Pasado simpleI lost my phone yesterday.
at 5 o'clock yesterday, while, this time last…Pasado continuoAt 5 I was serving a customer.
when + acción cortaLarga: pasado continuo
Corta: pasado simple		I was folding jeans when she called.
just, already, yet, resultado ahoraPresent perfectShe has just gone home.

Truco: si hay una fecha o un momento terminado (yesterday, last week), nunca uses el present perfect.
