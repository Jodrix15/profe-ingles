Presente continuo o presente simple

Unidad 8

En inglés hay dos presentes y no se pueden cambiar uno por otro. La pregunta clave es: ¿está pasando ahora mismo o pasa en general?

Continuo
I am doing		Simple
I do
¿Cuándo?Ahora, en este momentoEn general: siempre, a menudo, cada día, a veces
Formaam / is / are + verbo-ingverbo (+ -s con he/she/it)
En español"estoy trabajando""trabajo"
Pistasnow, at the moment, today, Look!, Listen!, Be quiet!every day, usually, always, often, never, on Saturdays
NegativaI'm not working · she isn't workingI don't work · she doesn't work

Un ejemplo con las dos

  • I work at Zara from Monday to Friday. → es mi trabajo, en general.
  • Sorry, I can't talk. I'm working. → ahora mismo.
  • Ana usually works at the till, but today she's working in the stockroom. → normalmente / hoy.
  • It rains a lot in winter. — Take an umbrella. It's raining.

Ojo: en español usamos el presente para todo

En español decimos "¿Qué haces?" tanto para "¿a qué te dedicas?" como para "¿qué estás haciendo?". En inglés son dos preguntas distintas:

  • What do you do? = ¿a qué te dedicas? — I'm a shop assistant.
  • What are you doing? = ¿qué estás haciendo (ahora)? — I'm folding T-shirts.
  • Please be quiet. I work. → Please be quiet. I'm working.
  • I'm working at Zara every Saturday. → I work at Zara every Saturday.

Verbos que no van en -ing

Unidad 8

Algunos verbos hablan de lo que piensas, sabes o quieres, no de una acción. Estos verbos van siempre en presente simple, aunque sea ahora mismo.

VerboSignificaEjemplo

  • I'm wanting a coffee. → I want a coffee.
  • Are you understanding me? → Do you understand me?
  • She is needing a smaller size. → She needs a smaller size.

El pasado de to be: was / were

Unidad 10

To be solo tiene dos formas en pasado. Sirven para "era, estaba, fue, estuvo, eras, estabas…".

am / is → was · are → were

AfirmativaNegativa
I / he / she / itI was tired.I wasn't tired. (was not)
we / you / theyThey were late.They weren't late. (were not)
  • I'm tired now. → I was tired last night.
  • The shop is quiet today. → The shop was very busy yesterday.
  • These jeans are €19.95 now. They were €29.95 last week.
  • When I was a child, I was scared of dogs.
  • Truco: I va con was, igual que con am: I were → I was. You siempre con were: you was → you were.
  • Was y were no se contraen en afirmativa: I's tired.

Palabras de pasado

yesterday (ayer) · last night (anoche) · last week / last Saturday / last year (la semana pasada, el sábado pasado, el año pasado) · this morning (esta mañana) · an hour ago / two days ago (hace una hora, hace dos días) · this time last year (el año pasado por estas fechas) · when I was a child (cuando era pequeño/a).

  • Ago va detrás: five minutes ago — ago five minutes.
  • Last va sin the: last week — the last week.

Igual que en presente: "tener" → to be

I was hungry (tenía hambre) · She was 22 last year (tenía 22 años) · We were cold (teníamos frío).

Nacer: I was born

"Nací" se dice con was: I was born in Barcelona. — I born in Barcelona. Where were you born? (¿Dónde naciste?)

Preguntas y respuestas cortas con was / were

Unidad 10

Igual que en presente: was / were pasa delante del sujeto. No se usa do.

(Where / Why / How…) + was / were + sujeto + resto?

AfirmativaPregunta
The shop was busy.Was the shop busy?
You were at work.Were you at work?
They were expensive.How much were they?
  • Why were you late this morning? — The traffic was bad.
  • How was…? = ¿qué tal fue…? How was your weekend? — It was great, thanks!
  • Was you at home? → Were you at home?

Respuestas cortas

SíNo
I / he / she / itYes, I was.No, I wasn't.
we / you / theyYes, they were.No, they weren't.
  • Ojo con you: si te preguntan Were you at work?, contestas con I: Yes, I was.
  • Was the jacket expensive? — No, it wasn't. · Were Sue and Steve at the party? — No, they weren't.
